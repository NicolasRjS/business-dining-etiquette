10 rules in 2 minutes

If you only have time for one thing, read this list. Each rule links to the page that explains it and cites its sources.

  1. Pick a restaurant you already know.

    Quiet, mid-priced and easy for your guest to reach. How to choose the place

  2. Ask about tastes and allergies first.

    A short question before the meal avoids an awkward menu. What to find out

  3. Reserve a table.

    Waiting in line with a guest makes you look unprepared. Reservations

  4. Let your guest order first.

    Then choose a dish in a similar price range. The price-matching rule

  5. Order food that is easy to eat.

    Leave the lobster and the spaghetti for another day. Ordering discreetly

  6. Know which plate and glass are yours.

    Bread plate on your left, glasses on your right. Explore the place setting

  7. Use the napkin only for your lips.

    If you step away, it goes on your chair, not on the table. Napkin rules

  8. Keep your phone off the table.

    It should never ring during the meal. Phones at the table

  9. Start with small talk, not business.

    But do not save the serious points for dessert. When to talk business

  10. Be polite to the people who serve you.

    Ask calmly, thank briefly. The rule is at least 170 years old. What Carreño wrote

Three places to start

The guide follows the order of a real meal. Read it in sequence or jump to what you need.
